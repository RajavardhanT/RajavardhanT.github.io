Patent-related Invention 1 • Rydberg Field Imaging

2D electromagnetic field imager using light-sheet fluorescence spectroscopy of Rydberg states

Role: One of the contributors to this invention.

This invention enables two-dimensional imaging of electric and magnetic fields using light-sheet fluorescence detection of Rydberg atoms in a vapor cell. By combining electromagnetically induced transparency (EIT) with imaging of planar laser-induced fluorescence, the technique provides minimally invasive, broadband, and SI-traceable field measurements with spatial resolution down to 160 μm and sensitivity to fields as low as 5 mV/cm. It allows imaging of both resonant and non-resonant RF electric fields from MHz to tens of GHz, as well as static magnetic fields, without the need for external calibration. The method offers a compact, scalable platform for high-resolution field mapping across scientific, industrial, and defense applications.

Patent-related Invention 2 • Rydberg RF Angle of Arrival

Rydberg Electrode Cell Angle of Arrival Estimator

Role: One of the contributors to this invention.

Deep subwavelength RF imaging with atomic Rydberg sensors has overcome fundamental limitations of traditional antennas and enabled ultra-wideband detection of omni-directional time varying fields all in a compact form factor. However, in most applications, Rydberg sensors require the use of a secondary strong RF reference field to serve as a phase reference. Here, we demonstrate a new type of Rydberg sensor for angle-of-arrival (AoA) sensing which utilizes subwavelength imaging of standing wave fields. By placing a metallic plate within the Rydberg cell, we can determine the AoA independent of the strength of incoming RF field and without requiring a secondary strong RF phase reference field.
