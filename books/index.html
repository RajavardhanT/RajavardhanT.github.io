Science, History & Life

Pendulum: Leon Foucault and the Triumph of Science

Amir D. Aczel

Einstein's Masterwork: 1915 and the General Theory of Relativity

John Gribbin with Mary Gribbin

It outlines the crux of Einstein's Annus Mirabilis papers and then his masterwork on general relativity.

Einstein and the Quantum

A. Douglas Stone

A fascinating account of Einstein's contributions to quantum theory and his influence on Bose, de Broglie, Schrödinger, Born, Heisenberg, and others.

The Day We Found the Universe

Marcia Bartusiak

A thrilling history of how our understanding of the universe changed through astronomy, including the scientific debates and personalities behind those discoveries.

Black Hole

Marcia Bartusiak

From Newtonian ideas to modern evidence for black holes, this book traces the long path toward one of the most remarkable concepts in physics.

Einstein: A Life in Science

Michael White & John Gribbin

A readable journey through Einstein's life with enough scientific detail to deepen one's appreciation of his intellectual achievements.

Michael Faraday and The Royal Institution

John M. Thomas

I read this on Prof. Andrew Zangwill's recommendation. It is a gem.

Longitude

Dava Sobel

A reminder of how many obstacles can stand between a scientific problem and a practical solution—even when the problem was known to people such as Newton.

The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari

Robin Sharma

I have read this multiple times. I may not put all of it into practice, but the book changed my life to some extent.

Dispatches from Planet 3

Marcia Bartusiak
